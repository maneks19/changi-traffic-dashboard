About the data & sources

Source: Passenger demand data.

2025–26 · Singapore markets · Airports combined by city

Demand reporting period: The source spreadsheet labels the dataset “Annual PAX Market Data (Year 2025–26)”. Figures represent annual volumes, not a two-year total. Exact start and end dates are not specified in its documentation. This period is separate from the nonstop-service snapshot dated 30 September 2026.

Passenger demand includes connecting journeys and covers both directions unless One way is selected. One-way estimates are half of both-direction demand; daily estimates divide annual demand by 365. Airports are combined into cities; each airport pair is counted once per cell and total. Country groupings keep territories separate.

Totals reflect the current filters. Passenger demand share uses total filtered Singapore demand. In unserved views, percentages use the full matching market, including served destinations; tiles and rankings use only unserved demand.

Nonstop coverage and airlines: snapshot as of 30 September 2026. Seasonal routes count as served; future-only routes count as unserved. Coverage status does not describe the passenger-demand itinerary.

The direction control above Annual and Daily applies to annual and daily passenger figures, including demand tiles; shares, fares, RPM and the 20,000-passenger eligibility threshold stay unchanged. Click any table header to sort all matching destinations in either direction. Choose 30, 50 or All destinations. Remaining destinations are combined in Others. Blue bars compare demand with the largest matching market across all destinations, regardless of the sort order. City-pair and service counts are calculated before destination grouping, including markets in Others. The unserved-city count and unserved-demand tile include only cities with more than 20,000 annual passengers in both directions, after combining airports. This tile threshold does not filter the destination table. Seasonal-only cities are included in cities served.

Fare per mile (RPM, USD per passenger-mile) is estimated fare revenue divided by passenger-miles. Grouped values use total fare revenue divided by total passenger-miles. Average fare and RPM are always shown in the table and can be sorted using their column headers.

For personal, non-commercial use only.

Region definitions

These are market-analysis groupings. Turkey and Cyprus are in Southern Europe. North America includes the US, Canada, Bermuda, Greenland, and Saint Pierre and Miquelon. Russia is grouped with Central Asia. Exclusions use the country, subregion or main region of each city before aggregation.

Groups include only markets matching the current view and filters. Each airport pair is counted once per cell and total. Country groupings keep territories separate.
